## Crestline Septic Service FAQs

**How often should I pump my septic tank in Crestline?**
Every three to five years for a typical household. Homes with garbage disposals, large families, or older smaller tanks should pump more often. Hillside lots do not change the schedule, but poor drainage punishes neglected systems faster.

**Does slope affect how long a drain field lasts?**
It can. Effluent follows gravity, so on sloped lots it may concentrate at the low end of the field and overload that section while the upper lines stay dry. Proper original design accounts for this, but older fields were not always graded well. An inspection can show whether the field is distributing evenly.

**How do I find my septic records?**
Contact the Jefferson County Department of Health. If a permit was issued for your address, they can provide the tank size, field layout, and install date. Many mid-century Crestline systems predate thorough records, in which case an inspection fills the gap.

**What does a septic inspection include?**
Tank structure, sludge and scum levels, baffles, and field absorption, ending with a written report on condition and remaining life. It is the single most useful service for an older system.

**Do I need a permit for septic repairs?**
The Jefferson County Department of Health issues permits for new installations and major repairs. A qualified crestline septic service manages the permit process as part of larger jobs so the work stays compliant.
