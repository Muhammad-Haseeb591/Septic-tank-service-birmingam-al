## Ensley Septic FAQs

**How do I know if my septic tank needs pumping?**
Slow drains throughout the house, sewage odors, gurgling toilets, and wet spots over the drain field are the signs. If it has been more than five years, assume it is due regardless of symptoms.

**What does septic pumping cost in Ensley?**
Most Birmingham area pump-outs run 250 to 550 dollars. Size, access, and fullness set the number. We quote before we pump, no surprises after.

**Can you repair my system instead of replacing it?**
Usually yes, at least as a first step. Baffles, filters, distribution boxes, and lines are all repairable. Replacement is the last resort, not the opening offer.

**Do I need a permit for septic work?**
The Jefferson County Department of Health permits septic work countywide. We handle permits for replacements and major work. Simple repairs and pump-outs do not need them.

**My tenant says the drains are slow. What should I do?**
Get it checked before it becomes a backup. Slow drains across fixtures point to the tank or field, and early fixes are cheap. Emergency backups cost more and upset tenants.
