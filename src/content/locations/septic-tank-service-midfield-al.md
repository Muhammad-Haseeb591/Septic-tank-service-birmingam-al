**How frequently should I pump?**

**Who issues septic permits around Midfield?**
The Jefferson County Department of Health, covering site evaluations, permits, and records.

**Does clay soil really matter?**
Yes. Slow-draining clay plus heavy rain is the top cause of field stress locally. Proper pumping cadence is your best countermeasure.

**Will additives extend time between pump-outs?**
No. Additives do not remove solids. Only pumping restores working capacity.

**What should I do during a sewage backup?**
Stop running water immediately, keep clear of the affected area, and call for service. Avoid chemical drain cleaners, which can harm septic components. Note which fixtures are affected and whether the trouble followed heavy rain; both details speed up the diagnosis.
