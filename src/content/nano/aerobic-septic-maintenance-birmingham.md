## FAQ

**How often does an aerobic system need service?**
Most manufacturers recommend service every 3 to 4 months. We offer quarterly plans that cover every required check and keep your documentation current.

**What happens if I skip maintenance?**
Alarms go off, spray heads clog, and untreated effluent can surface in your yard. In Jefferson County, neglected ATUs can also draw compliance notices.

**Do you service my brand of system?**
We service all major aerobic treatment unit brands. Call with your model number and we will confirm coverage.

**What do the alarms mean?**
High-water alarms signal pump or float problems. Aerator alarms mean the treatment process has stopped. Either way, call promptly: alarms exist to prevent surface discharge.

**Can I maintain my aerobic system myself?**
You can replace chlorine tablets and keep the area clear, but pumps, aerators, and electrical components need a trained technician. DIY electrical work on an ATU voids warranties and risks shock.
