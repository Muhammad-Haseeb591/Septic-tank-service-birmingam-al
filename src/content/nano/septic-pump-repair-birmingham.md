## Repair or Replace? How We Decide

Not every dead pump needs replacing. A pump that hums but does not move water may just have a jammed impeller or a stuck float. We test the motor windings, check the float sequence, and inspect the panel before recommending anything. If the pump is under ten years old and the motor tests good, repair is usually the smart money. If the housing is cracked or the motor is burned out, replacement is honest advice, not an upsell.

## Why Pumps Fail in Birmingham
