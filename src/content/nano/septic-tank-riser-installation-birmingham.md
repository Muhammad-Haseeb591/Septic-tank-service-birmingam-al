## Concrete vs Plastic Risers

Concrete risers match older concrete tanks and handle heavy loads well, making them the pick for driveways and traffic areas. High-density plastic risers are lighter, stack faster, and resist the corrosion that can affect concrete in aggressive soils. Both seal watertight when installed correctly. We recommend based on your tank material, lid location, and how the area above gets used, not on which costs us less to install.

## Risers Also Protect Your System

Buried lids crack under mowers and vehicles. A cracked lid lets rainwater flood the tank and lets dirt fall in, both of which shorten system life. Grade-level lids with safety screws keep kids and pets safe while keeping stormwater out. In a region with 50+ inches of annual rain, that protection pays for itself.
