## FAQ

**How often should I pump my tank?**
Every 3 to 5 years for most households. Larger families or heavy water users should pump every 3 years. Jefferson County's clay soil and high rainfall make the 3-year end of the range the safer bet.

**Are maintenance plans worth it?**
Yes, if you actually use them. A plan bundles scheduled pumping, annual inspections, and filter service at a discount versus buying each separately, and it keeps you on schedule automatically.

**What is the most expensive maintenance mistake?**
Skipping pump-outs. Solids escaping into the drain field cause the costliest failures in septic systems, and no additive or treatment reverses a clogged field.

**Should I use septic additives?**
Most additives do nothing your tank's bacteria cannot do alone, and some can harm the drain field. Regular pumping beats every bottle on the shelf.

**How long do septic systems last in Birmingham?**
A well-maintained conventional system lasts 25 to 40 years here. Red clay and high water tables punish neglected systems, but consistent pumping and inspections keep most systems healthy for decades.
